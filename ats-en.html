Real-estate photo enhancement pipeline (2026, EvPro)

A PHP 8.4 / Laravel 12 module for batch processing of property photos. The decision to enhance is driven by nine measured frame features (brightness, contrast, shadows, highlights, noise, sharpness and others) collapsed into a single improvement score, with eleven guard checks on top that block publication when quality regresses. A cascade of "local processing -> external generative model" draws an explicit line between what is done for free and what is paid for. Time and cost per unit of work were measured (about 25 seconds per frame locally and roughly three dollars for a batch of some sixty frames through the external model), which moved the quality conversation with the business from impressions to money.

Project methodology JD+PARA (2026, open source)

My own system for running technical projects: Johnny.Decimal for numbering, P.A.R.A. for separating projects and resources, MOC for links - with rules that let both a human and AI agents work on the same documentation. What makes it more than an organiser: a question, a decision and a knowledge note are separate entities with mandatory fields. A question must state what changes once it is answered; a decision must list rejected alternatives and the condition for revisiting it; a finding must state what would refute it. That makes a project handover-ready: the next person (or agent) doesn't redo work already done. In use across nine of my own projects: more than two hundred recorded questions and about a hundred and eighty fixed decisions. Starter kit published at github.com/Miniwe/jdpara.
